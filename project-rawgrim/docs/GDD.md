## Genre
Dark Fantasy Hero Management Auto-Battler

## Platforms
- Initial: Web
- Future: Windows and Android

## Gameplay
- Hero summoning
- Hero management
- Party formation
- Automated combat
- Tower progression
- Permadeath

## MVP
- 10 hero templates
- 4 classes
- 5 tower floors
- 5v5 combat
- 8 skills
- 1 boss

## Visual Direction
Dark fantasy, purple, black, and gold.
UI-first development.
No 3D assets required for the MVP.

## Hero Intelligence

Heroes use individual multidimensional personalities and deterministic Utility AI. Party strategy influences decisions according to each hero's discipline, but never bypasses combat validation. Heroes develop bounded contextual preferences from eligible completed battles, retain factual episodic memories, and form directional relationships from observable shared events. A deployed leader can improve strategy adherence according to effectiveness, teammate trust, respect, and individual discipline. Party chemistry diagnoses cohesion, coordination, conflict risk, and leadership stability without granting unexplained combat-stat bonuses. Sandbox and laboratory experiments remain isolated from player progression.

The accepted domain model, save migration, scoring bounds, and current limitations are documented in [AI_ARCHITECTURE.md](./AI_ARCHITECTURE.md).
