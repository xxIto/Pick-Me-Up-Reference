# Project Ascension
Version: 0.1

## Genre
Dark Fantasy Hero Management Auto-Battler

## Platforms
- Initial: Web
- Future: Windows and Android

## Gameplay
- Hero summoning
- Hero management
- Party formation
- Automated combat
- Tower progression
- Permadeath

## MVP
- 10 hero templates
- 4 classes
- 5 tower floors
- 5v5 combat
- 8 skills
- 1 boss

## Visual Direction
Dark fantasy, purple, black, and gold.
UI-first development.
No 3D assets required for the MVP.
